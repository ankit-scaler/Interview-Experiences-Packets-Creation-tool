Sections rail — three ways to make it stand out

Today the inactive items are dim grey on the page background and the current one is a faint tint, so the rail and the reading text read as one block. All three options fix that with full-contrast labels, a clearly different current item, and visible status for what you have finished. Click items to try them.

Option 1 · Panel

The rail gets its own surface and a "Sections · 6" header. Labels are full-strength text. The current section is a solid accent bar, so you can find it from across the screen. Smallest change from today.

Option 2 · Stepper

Sections are grouped into "Prepare" and "Rounds", joined by a line with a node per section: filled check when finished, pie when started, hollow when not. The current node glows. Best at showing where you are in the whole packet and what is left.

Option 3 · Tiles

Each section is its own card with a one-line summary ("12 min read", "18 questions · 30 min") and a progress bar. The current tile gets a thick accent border, tinted fill and a shadow. Takes the most room, but tells you what is inside before you click.
